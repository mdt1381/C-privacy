Privacy Policy for Chromachute

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Chromachute ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Game Analytics: Level completion times, game progress, joker usage, in-game statistics
  • Technical Data: Crash reports, performance metrics

2.2 Information You Provide

  • Purchase Information: When you make in-app purchases, payment information is processed by Google Play (we do not store payment details)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Providing and improving game functionality
  • Analyzing game performance and user experience
  • Processing in-app purchases
  • Displaying advertisements (via Google AdMob)
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google Play Services: For in-app purchases and authentication
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements (see AdMob Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase: For analytics and data storage (optional, only if enabled)

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data collection and use.

5. Advertising

We display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device information to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

We offer a "Remove Ads" in-app purchase to disable banner and interstitial ads (rewarded ads remain available for optional rewards).

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Game progress and statistics are stored locally on your device
  • Analytics data may be uploaded to Firebase (if configured)
  • We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy

7. Children's Privacy

Chromachute is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data (uninstall the app to remove local data)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

9. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
